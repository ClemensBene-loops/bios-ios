BIOS

Personal health data project

BIOS is a private, non-commercial, single-user application. It combines the operator's own health data (continuous glucose monitoring, insulin pump and Loop data, and WHOOP recovery, sleep and workout data) into one place and shows it in a personal iOS app. Its optional Google Calendar integration creates a dedicated calendar named "BIOS" and writes day-level health status events into it; it does not read or change any other calendar. BIOS has no other users and is not offered to the public.

Privacy policy

BIOS is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.
